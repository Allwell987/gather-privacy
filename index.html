Gather

Gather is The Wealth Reality Church's community app, helping members stay connected and take part in church life throughout the week.

What you can do with Gather

  • Read the Bible and daily devotionals, and save verse highlights.
  • Share prayer requests and take part in church groups and conversations.
  • Discover church events, RSVP, and receive optional reminders.
  • Support the church or app through the giving features.

About the app

Gather is provided by The Wealth Reality Church in Warri, Delta State, Nigeria. An account is required for community features; this information page and our privacy policy are available without signing in.
